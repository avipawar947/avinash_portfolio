My Journey

The journey Start with a strong foundation in technology through BCA and MCA,

Where I learned to think logically and solve problems. I became more curious about

How they think, what they need, and why something that looks simple can still feel

Difficult to use.. That curiosity led me into UI/UX, where problem-solving

And creativity started to come together. Over the years, I’ve worked across startups,

Growing teams, and larger organizations, learning something new at every stage.

From early client projects to leading design teams and building complex digital products.

Every experience has shaped the way And this journey is still evolving—one problem, one

Idea, and one meaningful experience at a time.
